# Playtest sheet

For a parent watching a 3 to 5 year old play on an Android phone or tablet. About 15 minutes. Fill in what you can, then paste the whole sheet back to Claude.

## Before you start
- Open https://avatar-coco-love.github.io/animal-mail-route/ in Chrome.
- Turn the volume up and the silent switch off.
- Optional: Chrome menu ⋮ → "Add to home screen" (or "Install app"), then open it from the home screen.
- Let the child play. Help only if they are stuck for a while, and note where.
